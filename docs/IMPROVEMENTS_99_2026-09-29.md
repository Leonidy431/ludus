DEFICITS: after merging duplicates, 55 real deficits remain (23 P0, 19 P1, 12 P2, 1 P3). The list was not padded to 99. Deficits are ranked by priority, then by how many gurus raised them. Merges: passions (6 gurus) → DEF-001; osmoglasie folded into DEF-012; the kliros/analoy/iconostasis graphics folded into DEF-025; the Isaac stillness timer folded into DEF-028; the hesychast cave folded into DEF-029.

RULES: there are 23 rules. No guru rule was dropped for contradicting TABOO 0 or 0.2. Operator proposals that were not accepted as rules: (a) 'the nimbus as a dynamic glow when the player's Ethos resonates' and 'AR rings'. Ethos is not one of the 7 attributes, and rings resemble a nimbus. Only light remains, and it is not tied to the player. (b) 'Protocol Zero' and log self-destruction. The operator cancelled both, and the opposite is now a rule: logs are kept.
